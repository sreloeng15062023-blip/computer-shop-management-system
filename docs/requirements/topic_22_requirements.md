# Topic 22: Computer Shop Management System
## Main Functional Requirements

### 1. User Authentication
- User Login & Logout
- Change Password & User Profile
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):
  - Admin
  - Manager
  - Sale Staff
  - Cashier
  - Technician

### 2. Dashboard
- Total Products & Available Stock
- Low Stock Alerts
- Today's Sales & Monthly Revenue
- Total Customers
- Active Repair Orders & Warranty Claims
- Best Selling Products
- Sales & Inventory Charts

### 3. Product Management
- Add, Update, Delete, and Search Products
- Upload Product Images
- Barcode / Serial Number Management
- Categories: Desktop Computers, Laptops, Gaming PCs, Monitors, Printers, Keyboards, Mouse, SSD/HDD, RAM, Graphics Cards, Motherboards, CPUs, Networking Devices, UPS, Accessories.
- Product Details: SKU, Barcode, Serial Number, Product Name, Brand, Category, Model, Specifications, Purchase Price, Selling Price, Stock Quantity, Warranty Period, Product Status (Available, Out of Stock, Discontinued).

### 4. Brand Management
- Add, Update, Delete, Search Brand (e.g. Dell, HP, Lenovo, ASUS, Acer, MSI, Apple, Intel, AMD, NVIDIA, Logitech, Canon, Epson).

### 5. Supplier Management
- Add, Update, Delete, Search Supplier
- Purchase History tracking
- Supplier Details: ID, Name, Contact Person, Phone, Email, Address.

### 6. Customer Management
- Register, Update, Delete, Search Customer
- Purchase History & Warranty History
- Loyalty Membership & Points tracking
- Customer Details: ID, Full Name, Phone, Email, Address, Membership Level, Loyalty Points.

### 7. Purchase Management
- Create Purchase Order, Receive Products, Update Purchase, Cancel Purchase, Purchase History.

### 8. Inventory Management
- Stock In, Stock Out, Stock Adjustment, Inventory History.
- Low Stock Alerts, Barcode Tracking, Serial Number Tracking.

### 9. Sales Management (POS)
- Product Search, Cart, Quantity Update.
- Apply Discount, Apply Coupon, Apply Loyalty Points, Calculate Tax.
- Process Payment (Cash, Credit/Debit Card, Bank Transfer, QR Payment).
- Print Receipt, Product Return & Exchange.

### 10. Repair Service Management
- Register Repair Request, Device Diagnosis.
- Assign Technician, Update Repair Status:
  - Received -> Diagnosing -> Waiting for Parts -> Repairing -> Testing -> Ready for Pickup -> Completed / Cancelled.
- Record Spare Parts Used, Estimate Repair Cost, Complete Repair, Print Service Receipt.

### 11. Warranty Management
- Register Product Warranty, Warranty Verification.
- Warranty Claim Processing, Expiry Tracking, Warranty Service History.

### 12. Payment & Invoice Management
- Generate Invoice, Record Payment, Print Invoice.
- Payment History, Outstanding Balance, Refund Management.

### 13. Employee Management
- Add, Update, Delete Employee.
- Attendance Management, Work Schedule, Salary Information.
- Roles: Manager, Sales Staff, Cashier, Technician, Storekeeper.

### 14. Report Management
- Reports: Product Inventory, Daily Sales, Monthly Sales, Purchase, Customer, Supplier, Repair Service, Warranty, Revenue, Profit & Loss, Best Selling Products, Low Stock.
- Export formats: Print, PDF, Excel.

### 15. Notification System
- Low Stock Alert, Warranty Expiry Reminder, Repair Completion Notification, Promotion Notification, Purchase Order Reminder.

### 16. Settings
- Shop Information, Tax Configuration, Currency Settings, Business Hours, Backup & Restore Database.
